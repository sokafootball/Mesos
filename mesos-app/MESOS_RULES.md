# Mesos — Official Game Rules

**Designers:** Yaniv Kahana & Simone Luciani  
**Publisher:** Cranio Creations / Gravitix Games  
**Players:** 2–5  
**Age:** 10+  
**Duration:** 20–50 minutes

---

## Overview

Mesos is set in the Mesolithic era — thousands of years ago, when humankind began its journey toward civilization. Players act as **tribal leaders**, guiding their people through crucial stages of prehistory. By collecting tribe members, constructing buildings, and responding to events, players compete to earn the most **Prestige Points (PP)** by the end of the game.

---

## Components

- Tribe cards (Era I, II, and III, plus Final Event cards)
- Building cards (one set per Era)
- Offer Track board
- Turn Order tile
- Totem pawns (one per player)
- Food tokens
- Prestige Point (PP) tokens
- Event cards

---

## Setup

1. **Offer Track:** Place the Offer Track board in the center of the table. Arrange Offer tiles in order according to the player count. Place the Turn Order tile next to them.

2. **Tribe Card Deck:** Sort Tribe cards into Era I, Era II, and Era III decks. Place the Final Event cards at the **bottom**, then shuffle in Era III cards, then Era II, and Era I on **top** — creating a single draw deck.

3. **Display:** Draw cards from the deck to fill the display:
   - **Bottom row:** number of players + 1 cards
   - **Top row:** number of players + 4 cards
   - Any **Event cards** drawn go face-up **above** the Offer tiles, not in the display.

4. **Buildings:** Prepare Building card piles for each Era separately.

5. **Starting Resources:** Each player takes their Totem pawn. Distribute starting Food tokens as indicated by player count.

6. **First Player:** Determine the starting player randomly.

---

## Game Structure

The game is played over **10 rounds**, divided into **three Eras**:

| Era     | Rounds      |
| ------- | ----------- |
| Era I   | Rounds 1–4  |
| Era II  | Rounds 5–7  |
| Era III | Rounds 8–10 |

Each Era introduces new Tribe and Building cards with more powerful abilities and higher Prestige Point values.

---

## Each Round

### Phase 1 — Totem Placement

Starting with the first player and continuing clockwise, each player places their **Totem pawn** on one of the available spaces on the Offer Track.

- **Left spaces** act earlier (better card priority) but allow **fewer cards** to be taken.
- **Right spaces** act later (worse card priority) but allow **more cards** to be taken.

Turn order for the next round is determined by the position of the Totem pawns on the track (leftmost = first).

### Phase 2 — Card Drafting

In the **new turn order**, players take the number of cards allowed by their Offer Track space. Cards may be taken from either row of the display.

- **Tribe Cards:** Add to your tribe (your personal tableau). Most tribe members have a specialty (Hunter, Gatherer, Builder, Healer, etc.) and a Prestige Point value.
- **Building Cards:** Pay the required Food cost to construct a Building and add it to your tableau. Buildings provide ongoing abilities or end-game scoring bonuses.

After all players have drafted, **refill the display** from the deck to restore it to its original size.

### Phase 3 — Event Resolution

At specific points during the game (four times per Era), an **Event card** is resolved. Events may:

- **Reward** players who have met certain conditions (e.g., having enough Hunters, Healers, or a specific Building).
- **Penalize** players who have not prepared sufficiently.

Events are telegraphed in advance — they are visible above the Offer Track from the moment they are drawn, giving players time to prepare.

---

## Tribe Members

Each Tribe card represents a person with a **specialty**:

| Specialty | Role                                             |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Hunter    | Valuable for hunting Events and scoring          |
| Gatherer  | Provides Food for constructing Buildings         |
| Builder   | Reduces Building construction costs              |
| Healer    | Protects against negative Events                 |
| Elder     | High Prestige Point value, special abilities     |
| Child     | Low cost; grows into more powerful tribe members |

Tribe members also have a **Food cost** to recruit and a **PP value** for end-game scoring.

---

## Buildings

Buildings require Food tokens to construct. Each building may provide:

- An **immediate effect** when built
- A **passive ability** throughout the game
- A **scoring bonus** at end-game based on your tribe composition

Building cards are split by Era; more powerful buildings become available in Era II and III.

---

## Food Economy

Food tokens are the primary resource:

- Gained from Gatherers and certain Events
- Spent to recruit high-cost Tribe members and construct Buildings
- Some events penalize players with insufficient Food

---

## Era Advancement

When the game transitions between Eras:

1. Remove all Era I (or II) cards remaining in the display and from the deck.
2. Replenish the display with the appropriate Era cards.
3. New, more powerful Building cards become available.

---

## Endgame & Scoring

The game ends after **Round 10** (the final round of Era III). Players then total their **Prestige Points**:

| Source                                        | PP                      |
| --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Tribe member PP values                        | As printed on each card |
| Building end-game bonuses                     | Varies by building      |
| Prestige Point tokens collected during Events | As earned               |

The player with the **most Prestige Points wins**. In case of a tie, the tied player with the most Food tokens wins. If still tied, the tied player with the most Tribe cards wins.

---

## Strategy Tips

- **Balance your tribe:** Events reward specific specialties. A diverse tribe is resilient; a specialized tribe can dominate certain Events.
- **Watch upcoming Events:** Plan your drafts around the visible Event cards to ensure you are prepared.
- **Buildings compound:** Strong early Buildings can snowball into large end-game bonuses.
- **Offer Track positioning:** Going early gives card choice; going late gives card quantity. Adapt to what's available.
- **Food management:** Don't over-recruit at the expense of Food needed for Buildings and Event requirements.

---

## Quick Reference

**Round sequence:**

1. Totem Placement (choose Offer Track space)
2. Card Drafting (in turn order)
3. Refill display
4. Resolve Event (if applicable at end of this round)

**Game end:** After Round 10 → Count all Prestige Points → Most PP wins.
